System kaise bana hai

Koi screen ittefaq se nahi chhupti. Screen tab dikhti hai jab yeh chaar cheezein razi hon — isi tarteeb mein dekhein.

1 · Business typeDukan kya hai: mart, pharmacy, restaurant, wholesale…Admin, ek dafa
2 · ModulesDukan kya kar sakti hai: POS, Inventory, Dine-in…Admin, dukan ke hisaab se
3 · PlanKitna de rahi hai aur kitni gunjaish: branches, staff, tills, products.Admin · plan kabhi module band nahi karta
4 · BandaIs user ko kya ijazat hai (permissions).Dukan ka malik, har staff ke liye

Misaal: pharmacy mein Dine-in nahi (module). Mart mein nuskhe ka register nahi (business type). Cashier ko Reports nahi (banda). Chauthi branch nahi ban rahi (plan).

Roz ka flow, trade-wise

Yeh tarteeb asli hai — pehle wala qadam agle ko data deta hai. Har trade ka safar asal UI par chala kar dekha gaya hai.

Mart / Grocery

  1. Ek dafa setupSettings (Business, Tax, Receipt) → Products ya Import → Categories → Suppliers → Staff.
  2. Maal aayaPurchases → Purchase order → Receive. Stock barh gaya, supplier ka udhaar chadha, cost naye rate se blend hui.
  3. SubahPOS → Open shift (float likhein).
  4. BikriScan → cart → Tender / Pay (Cash · Card · Wallet · Khata · Split) → raseed. Stock ghata, Sales mein aaya, khata/points update.
  5. WapsiSales → sale kholen → Refund. Maal shelf par wapas, paisa drawer se.
  6. KharchaDrawer → cash out, ya Expense Manager → Expenses. Cashbook mein dikhta hai.
  7. ShaamPOS → Close shift (gin kar) → Day & banking → din band, bank deposit.
  8. HaftaInventory → Needs reordering → order banayen · Stocktake · Disposals (expire maal).
  9. MaheenaReports (Overview, Margins, Tax) · Subscription.

Pharmacy

  1. Maal aayaPurchase order receive karte waqt har dawa ka batch number aur expiry likhna lazmi hai.
  2. BikriPOS pehle wo batch deta hai jo pehle expire hoga. Schedule wali dawa par nuskha (Rx) manga jata hai; same-salt ka mutabadil till par milta hai.
  3. RegisterDispensary → Dispensing register: kis ko kya diya. Batch recall: ek batch kahan kahan gaya.
  4. ExpiryQareeb-ul-expiry ka alert → Disposals → 'returned to supplier' (To claim) ya 'written off'.
  5. Strip / tabletPack unit: Box = 10 strips, Strip = 10 tablets — line par Sale Unit se chunein.

Restaurant / Cafe

  1. MenuProducts = dishes. Sizes (Small/Large), modifiers, recipe (ingredients se cost aur stock).
  2. Dine-inDine-in → table → Menu se order → Send to kitchen → Kitchen board → Food ready → Bill please → settle (split bhi).
  3. Takeaway / Cafe counterPOS se order; Kitchen ko slip (KOT). Cafe ko tables ki zaroorat nahi — POS + Kitchen kaafi.
  4. Khatam ho gayaDish 'sold out' (86) — till, waiter aur online teeno par band.
  5. Tips aur dinTip alag likhi jati hai · Close shift · Day & banking.

Clothing brand (Retail + warehouse)

  1. ProductsHar design ke sizes/colours — har size ka apna stock aur barcode. Barcode labels print karein.
  2. WarehouseBranches & warehouses → Add → Warehouse. Warehouse par till nahi hota, sirf stock.
  3. Maal aayaPurchase order → Deliver to: Central Warehouse → Receive into warehouse.
  4. Branch ko bhejnaInventory → Stock transfers → New transfer (warehouse → branch) → Send (raste mein) → branch Receive kare; kam nikle to settle.
  5. Kahan kitnaInventory → Stock by place: har design har jagah; 'bring it' ka link.
  6. BikriBranch ke POS par size chun kar; exchange/refund Sales se. Reports branch ke hisaab se.

Wholesale / Distributor

  1. RateProduct ki Price = dealer rate. Pack: Carton = 12 apni qeemat par. Quantity break: 60 ya zyada par sasta — till khud lagata hai.
  2. DealersCustomers → + Add customer → Credit limit. Wahi dealer ka khata hai.
  3. QuotationQuotes & Advances → quotation → Convert → invoice (on account).
  4. BikriPOS/New sale → line par Sale Unit: Carton → Tender: Khata. Limit se upar jaye to inkaar, raqam ke sath.
  5. WasooliCustomers → dealer → payment. Khata ghat jata hai.
  6. GodownWarehouse 'Deliveries come here' — naye orders wahin aate hain; dukan ko transfer.

Bill kaise banta hai

Dukan ka bill = plan ki qeemat + us dukan ke add-ons. Add-on woh module hai jo dukan ke paas hai magar us ke plan mein shamil nahi. Admin use on karta hai to woh khud bill par aa jata hai — koi custom plan nahi banta. Saalana plan par add-on 12 maheene ka lagta hai.

Plan ki qeemat Plans screen par likhi jati hai; add-on ki qeematon ko jor kar nahi banti. Qeemat barhane se agla bill badalta hai, jo ada ho chuka woh nahi.

Sawal-jawab

Jo sawal baar baar pooche gaye.

Kya POS walay ke liye Expense Manager zaroori hai?

Zaroori nahi. Till, shift, raseed, Sales, Day & banking aur bikri ki Reports is ke baghair mukammal chalti hain. Sirf bechne wali dukan ise chhor sakti hai.

Lene se kya milta hai: Cashbook (din ka sara paisa andar/bahar ek jagah), Expenses (kiraya, bijli, tankhwah — aur drawer se nikla cash), Income (bikri ke ilawa aamdani), Ledger, Budgets, aur Reports mein asal munafa (bikri − maal ki cost − kharche). Jo malik "aaj kitna bacha" dekhna chahta hai us ke liye yeh lena chahiye. Standard plan mein shamil hai; Basic par add-on.

Wholesale kis business type mein aata hai?

Apni type hai: Wholesale & Distribution — jo sirf maal bana kar/la kar dukanon ko bechte hain. Public marketplace par nahi hote. Product ki Price hi dealer rate hai, Carton apni qeemat par, zyada miqdar par rate khud kam, dealer ka khata credit limit ke sath. Retail dukan jo thora wholesale bhi karti hai woh Retail hi rahe — "wholesale price" aur customer group kaafi hain.

Kya warehouse compulsory hai? Kya yeh add-on hai?

Compulsory nahi. Compulsory sirf ek cheez hai: har karobar ki Main branch, jo khud ban jati hai. Zyada tar dukanein sab kuch dukan mein hi rakhti hain — un ke liye kuch nahi badla: maal branch par aata hai, wahin rehta hai, wahin bikta hai.

Add-on/module nahi — plan ki gunjaish hai ("kitne warehouse bana sakte ho"). Banana zaroori nahi. Paise lene hon to do tareeqe mojood hain: plan par Warehouses = 0 rakhein, ya kisi dukan ko extra warehouse capacity qeemat ke sath dein (jaise extra staff/branch) — woh us ke bill par alag dikhta hai.

Jab tak doosri jagah na bane, panel us ke baare mein kuch nahi dikhata: na Stock by place, na Stock transfers, na Sales mein Branch ka column, na upar branch ka switch. Doosri jagah (branch ya warehouse) bante hi yeh sab khud aa jata hai — Shopify, Square, Zoho aur Odoo mein bhi yehi tareeqa hai.

Warehouse kaise chalta hai?

Branches & warehouses mein jagah ki qism "Warehouse" chunein. Warehouse sirf maal rakhta hai — wahan till, shift ya table nahi khul sakta. Purchase order par "Deliver to" se maal seedha warehouse (ya seedha branch) mein aata hai; Stock transfers se branch ko jata hai (Send → raste mein → Receive, kam nikle to settle); Stock by place batata hai kahan kitna hai. Warehouse zaroori nahi — maal seedha branch par bhi aa sakta hai. Plan batata hai kitne warehouse ho sakte hain.

Ek screen nazar nahi aa rahi — kya karein?

Isi tarteeb mein dekhein: (1) Simple view to nahi? Full view ya gear ⚙. (2) Settings → Your modules mein woh module on hai? (3) Us user ke paas ijazat hai? (Staff). (4) Us business type ki screen hai? Module off ho to screen menu se, URL se aur server se — teeno jagah band hoti hai.

Kya plan badalne se koi module band ho jata hai?

Kabhi nahi. Modules dukan ke hote hain, plan ke nahi. Plan sirf qeemat aur gunjaish badalta hai. Chhote plan par jane se jo cheezein hain woh rehti hain; bas hadd se upar nayi nahi banti.

Yeh file kisi server par nahi — sirf aap ke computer par hai. Browser mein kholein ya print karein (Ctrl/Cmd + P). Screens ki tafseeli madad panel ke andar Help Centre mein hai.
