# What the plan says, to an office

**2026-10-10 · the third of the items left after the queue**

A plan's description is the platform's own sentence about it, typed on the
plan — and written about shops: *"One shop, one counter. Everything a single
till needs, and two years of history."* The subscription page printed it as
written, to everybody — including a business that bought only the books, on
the one page that says what it is paying for. The rest of that page had been
taught to speak to it in its own terms; this line had not.

`planSays(kind, description)` in `shop/subscriptionRows.ts`:

- a business that sells is told all of it, as written;
- one that does not is told the SENTENCES that are not about selling, and
  nothing in place of the ones that are. "A few branches and a team. Offline
  selling, and five years of history." becomes "A few branches and a team."
- a description written for an organisation ("Sized to the organisation…")
  comes through whole.

Nothing is reworded. A description is the platform's to write; an office is
simply not read the parts about a till it does not have. What its plan gives
it is in the figures underneath, which were already its own.

Not chosen: a second description per plan for non-selling businesses. One
more box on every plan, for one trade, that somebody would leave empty.
