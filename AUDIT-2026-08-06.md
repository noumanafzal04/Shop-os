## Verdict first

The platform is **substantially built and genuinely solid at its core**. The money paths — sale
pricing, tax, stock decrement, drawer reconciliation, returns, credit, ledger — are
server-authoritative, transactional and densely tested. 311 tenant API routes; only 5 have no
caller in the panel. Of the 53 gaps in the 2026-08-05 POS sweep, the large majority have since
shipped.

What it is **not** yet is uniformly finished across all eight business types. The weakness is not
in the engine, it is at the **seams**: where the type registry meets the tenant's actual module
map, and where a built-and-tested endpoint meets a screen that never calls it.

Rough completeness, by trade:

| Type | State | What's missing |
|---|---|---|
| `mart` / `retail` | **~95%** | cash rounding; POS quick-keys |
| `pharmacy` | **~95%** | — (substitution, register, recall, Rx, FEFO all shipped) |
| `food` | **~90%** | waiter report unwired; table reorder unwired |
| `petroleum` | **~90%** | Unit 11 shipped; forecourt polish |
| `automotive` | **~80%** | **cannot enable serial/warranty at all** (F2) |
| `services` | **~75%** | **cannot sell a physical product** (F1); booking deliberately out of scope |
| `finance` | **~65%** | **income missing from profit** (F3); no expense edit (F4) |

## P0 — proven defects

### F1 · A tenant granted a module still can't use it (`itemTypesFor` reads the wrong source)

`BusinessTypes::itemTypesFor()` ([BusinessTypes.php:491-531](shopos-backend/app/Support/BusinessTypes.php#L491-L531))
decides what a shop may put in its catalog by reading **the static type template** —
`$t['features']['products']` — not **the tenant's own module map**. `StoreProductRequest::withValidator`
([StoreProductRequest.php:186-192](shopos-backend/app/Http/Requests/Catalog/StoreProductRequest.php#L186-L192))
then rejects anything outside that list.

So the documented upgrade path does not work. The registry's own comment says a salon or workshop
"that also sells parts/retail turns them on per-tenant" ([BusinessTypes.php:195-197](shopos-backend/app/Support/BusinessTypes.php#L195-L197)),
and `services` is even seeded with a **`Retail Products`** product category — for items it can
never create.

**PROVEN.** Three tenants, module granted, product creation attempted:

```
[services + products] 422  item_type: "This item type isn't available for your business type."
[finance  + products] 422  item_type: "This item type isn't available for your business type."
[food     + services] 422  item_type: "This item type isn't available for your business type."
```

The failure is *silent in the worst way*: `feature:products` passes, the sidebar draws the Catalog,
the product form opens — and the item-type dropdown is empty. The merchant sees a working screen
that cannot save.

**Fix:** `itemTypesFor(string $code, ?Tenant $tenant = null)` — fall back to the template only when
no tenant is in context (it is also called for the `/business-types` picker, where there is none).
Existing tests at `BusinessTypeVisibilityTest:470-500` pin the template behaviour and must keep
passing for the no-tenant call.

### F2 · The auto trade cannot turn on serial + warranty — the one trade that needs it

Serial capture works end-to-end and is driven by the product flag, not the type: the POS renders the
IMEI chip from `l.tracks_serial` ([PosPage.tsx:1529-1534](shopos-admin-and-user-panel/src/modules/pos/pages/PosPage.tsx#L1529-L1534)),
and the backend route carries no type gate ([routes/api.php:440-444](shopos-backend/routes/api.php#L440-L444)).

Two UI conditions fence it off from `automotive`:

- the product form's **Serial & warranty** section is `businessType === "retail"` only
  ([ProductFormPage.tsx:652](shopos-admin-and-user-panel/src/modules/catalog/pages/ProductFormPage.tsx#L652));
- the **Warranty lookup** menu item is `businessType === "retail"` only
  ([AppSidebar.tsx:234-236](shopos-admin-and-user-panel/src/layout/AppSidebar.tsx#L234-L236)).

A battery is the single most warranty-claimed item in the Pakistani auto aftermarket. Today a tyre
and battery shop can neither record the serial nor take the claim. The capability is 100% built.

**Fix:** widen both conditions to `retail | automotive` (arguably `petroleum` too — the forecourt
sells batteries). One-line change each; add a test that an automotive tenant can flag
`tracks_serial` and sell against it.

### F3 · Income is absent from profit — the Cashbook and the Reports screen contradict each other

`DashboardService` contains the string `income` **zero times**. `ReportService::summary` computes
`net_profit = revenue − cogs − expenses` ([ReportService.php:81](shopos-backend/app/Services/ReportService.php#L81)),
and `revenue` is sales only. Meanwhile `cashbook()` correctly computes
`money_in = sales + manual income` ([ReportService.php:356-358, 427](shopos-backend/app/Services/ReportService.php#L356-L358)).

**PROVEN.** One `finance` tenant. Rs 300,000 income, Rs 80,000 expense, same month:

```
GET /cashbook        →  other_income 300000 · money_in 300000 · money_out 80000 · net  220000  ✅
GET /reports/summary →  revenue 0 · expenses 80000 ·                  net_profit  -80000  ❌
GET /dashboard       →  revenue 0 · expenses 80000 ·                      profit  -80000  ❌
```

Two screens in the same module, one click apart, disagreeing by the entire income of the business —
and the sign is inverted. The Reports page **does** render this: for a books-only tenant it draws a
`Net` card straight from `net_profit` ([ReportsPage.tsx:142](shopos-admin-and-user-panel/src/modules/expenses/pages/ReportsPage.tsx#L142)),
so a consultant Rs 220,000 up is shown **Rs −80,000**, every month, on the headline screen of the
one module they bought.

The dashboard's wrong `profit` is currently not *rendered* — `KpiRow` draws the profit tile only
`if (caps.sells)` ([KpiRow.tsx:155](shopos-admin-and-user-panel/src/modules/dashboard/components/shop/KpiRow.tsx#L155)) —
but it is in the API payload for any other consumer, and the books-only strip shows **spending
only**: `Spent This Month`, `Spent Last 7 Days`. A books-only dashboard never mentions income at all.

This also under-reports every selling type: an owner investment or supplier refund never reaches
profit. Sales dominate there, so it reads as a small understatement rather than an inversion.

**Fix:** join `incomes` in `ReportService::summary` and `DashboardService` (the correct query
already exists in `cashbook()`), publish `other_income` in both totals, and add an income tile to
the books-only KPI strip. Then assert cashbook.net == summary.net_profit for a books-only tenant —
the same "two depths, one truth" invariant `LedgerTest` already enforces against the Cashbook.

| Layer | Expenses | Income |
|---|---|---|
| Route | `PUT /expenses/{id}` ✅ | `PUT /incomes/{id}` ✅ |
| Backend tests | 4 (incl. the shift-linkage rules) ✅ | ✅ |

| Panel hook | `useExpenseMutations().update` ✅ | ✅ |
| **A button** | **✗ never destructured** | ✅ `Edit` ([IncomePage.tsx:221](shopos-admin-and-user-panel/src/modules/income/pages/IncomePage.tsx#L221)) |

[ExpensesPage.tsx:121](shopos-admin-and-user-panel/src/modules/expenses/pages/ExpensesPage.tsx#L121) reads
`const { create, remove, attach, detach } = useExpenseMutations()` — `update` is left on the floor.

You can edit an income but not an expense, in the same module, on sibling screens. A shop files
twenty expenses for every income, so the correction path is missing from the busier half. The only
recovery is delete-and-retype — and if the shift has closed that is refused too (`EXPENSE_SETTLED`),
leaving yesterday's typo permanently uncorrectable.

`updateRecurring` is likewise present in the service and called by nothing: when rent goes from
40k to 45k the template must be deleted and rebuilt, losing the schedule.

### F5 · Deactivating a category does nothing it is supposed to do — and does something it shouldn't

The third pass chose *deactivate over delete* so a year of history keeps its name. Both halves of
that are inverted in the UI:

- **Entry forms offer inactive categories.** Add expense ([:325](shopos-admin-and-user-panel/src/modules/expenses/pages/ExpensesPage.tsx#L325)),
  Add recurring ([:518](shopos-admin-and-user-panel/src/modules/expenses/pages/ExpensesPage.tsx#L518)) and
  Add income ([IncomePage.tsx:260](shopos-admin-and-user-panel/src/modules/income/pages/IncomePage.tsx#L260))
  all map the raw list with no `is_active` filter. The backend doesn't check it either —
  `StoreExpenseRequest` validates tenant + not-deleted only
  ([:22-27](shopos-backend/app/Http/Requests/Expense/StoreExpenseRequest.php#L22-L27)). So turning a
  category off does not stop new entries at either layer.
- **The filter bar hides them.** Both pages filter `is_active` before building the chip list
  ([ExpensesPage.tsx:200](shopos-admin-and-user-panel/src/modules/expenses/pages/ExpensesPage.tsx#L200),
  [IncomePage.tsx:170](shopos-admin-and-user-panel/src/modules/income/pages/IncomePage.tsx#L170)) — so the
  history filed under a retired category becomes unfindable by filter, which is the one thing
  deactivation was meant to protect.

Exactly backwards in both places. Swap them, and reject an inactive category server-side on create.

### F6 · Five endpoints with no caller in the panel

Checked systematically: every one of the 311 tenant routes against the whole panel source. 16 came

| Endpoint | What is stranded |

| `GET /restaurant/reports/waiters` | Waiter performance. Tickets already carry `waiter_id` and the panel assigns them. |
| `POST /restaurant/tables/reorder` | Floor layout ordering. |
| `GET`/`DELETE /auth/sessions[/{id}]` | Active-device list and remote sign-out — a security feature with no screen. |

## P2 — should exist, doesn't

- **No cash rounding.** `round_off` appears nowhere. Sub-rupee coins are out of circulation here and
  most counters settle to the nearest 5. Today a Rs 1,247.63 bill demands exact tender and produces
  change that cannot be given; the shortfall lands in the drawer variance — the very number the
  shift exists to protect. (Tips **are** shipped: `tip_amount`.)
- **No POS quick-keys / favourites.** Food gets an image grid; a mart selling loose items with no
  barcode has search only.
- **No on-screen numeric keypad** for quantity/weight/tender. `TillLock` has one for the PIN, so the
  component pattern already exists. A touch-only terminal can't key a weight.
- **Warranty is lookup-only** — no claim intake, so the desk can answer "is it covered?" and then
  has nowhere to record that someone brought it back.
- **No relief cover** on a lane (a 10-minute break stops it; handover moves the whole drawer).
- ~~No appointment booking for `services`.~~ **NOT being built — decided 2026-08-06.** A service
  business lists what it does and what it costs; customers see the menu and the price. There is no
  booking system and none is planned, so `reservations` defaulting off for that type is the right
  answer rather than a provisional one.
- **Customer display** is a `HardwareDevice` type with no render surface.
- **No training mode.**

## Business-type linkage — the structural notes

**The trade axis is enforced in the panel only.** The backend gates on MODULE and PERMISSION
everywhere; the only place `business_type` changes a server answer is `itemTypesFor` (F1). Vehicles,
warranty, pharmacy and portfolio are feature-gated server-side and type-gated *only in the sidebar*.
That fails safe (no 403 surprises) but means a trade screen's visibility rule lives in exactly one
place, with nothing behind it.

**`auto_workshop` is a category under two different types.** Both `services`
([BusinessTypes.php:207](shopos-backend/app/Support/BusinessTypes.php#L207)) and `automotive`
([:237](shopos-backend/app/Support/BusinessTypes.php#L237)) offer it, and `services` lists "Auto
Workshop" among its examples. Pick the wrong one at tenant creation and you get no vehicle register,
no plate lookup, no parts (F1) — a materially different product from the same self-description.
Either drop it from `services`, or resolve the trade screens from `business_category` as well as
`business_type`.

**Setup calls every tenant a shop.** [ShopSetupPage.tsx](shopos-admin-and-user-panel/src/modules/shop/pages/ShopSetupPage.tsx)
asks "tell us where your **shop** is", **Shop location** (map pin), **Shop street address**, with
city required. A Finance Manager tenant is a freelancer, an NGO, a software house — the first screen
they ever see asks a question they have no answer to. Not blocking (the pin is optional) but it is
the first impression for 1 of 8 types.

# P2, first two — 2026-08-06

Backend **1241** green / 5196 assertions (+12) · panel **102** green · all gates clean.

### Cash rounding — the drawer variance stops lying

A bill of Rs 1,238.15 has no exact cash tender: sub-rupee coins don't circulate
and plenty of counters can't break a five. The till demanded the exact figure
and produced change nobody could hand over, so the difference went into the
**drawer variance** — a few paisa per cash sale, until a real Rs 200 shortage
was indistinguishable from a month of accumulated change. That defeats the one
number a shift exists to produce.

New `cash_rounding` setting (0 / 1 / 5 / 10, **ships off** — a shop reconciling
to the paisa must not silently start rounding), `App\Support\CashRounding`, and
`sales.rounding_adjustment`.

Two rules carry it, both tested:

- **`total` never moves.** Tax is computed on it. A settlement convenience that
  shifts a tax figure is a worse bug than the one it fixes. What rounds is the
  amount DUE IN CASH; the gap is stored on its own so the receipt can print it
  and a month of them can be added back up.
- **Only cash rounds.** A card terminal takes 1,238.15 without complaint and a
  khata balance is a number in a ledger, not coins in a hand. Any non-cash
  tender on the sale ⇒ exact.

Ties go **down**, in the customer's favour. `DrawerMath` needed no change at
all — it reads tendered − change, so putting rounding in "what is due" makes the
expected cash follow for free. The test that matters: a correctly counted
drawer reports **variance 0**, not 37 paisa.

Receipt prints `Rounding` + a `To pay` line under the total. POS shows the
rounded figure it will actually collect, with the bill and the adjustment broken
out beneath.

### On-screen number pad — the till works without a keyboard

Most of these counters will run this on a cheap Android tablet on a stand. There
was no way to enter a number without the OS keyboard, which on a kiosk browser
often never appears and otherwise covers the figures being read. A weight was
worse than awkward: 1.250 kg at a step of one gram is 1,250 taps on the stepper.

New `NumPad` component; per-device toggle in `terminalStore` beside the lane
(whether you need a keypad is a fact about the hardware, not about who logged
in). Drives the cash tender, and the cart's quantity box opens a pad — decimals
only for weight-sold lines, since "3.5 packets" is not a thing.

Also cleared the four pre-existing `no-unused-vars` eslint errors in `PosPage`
while in the file; POS now lints clean.

# P2, the rest — 2026-08-06

Backend **1258** green / 5288 assertions (+17) · panel **102** green · all gates clean.

### Customer display — the control is withdrawn, not faked

`HardwareDevice::TYPES` includes `customer_display` and the panel built its
dropdown from every key, so a merchant could register one, name it, give it a
connection and save it — against a peripheral no code path has ever written a
character to. Identical shape to the *Show logo* toggle with no upload: not an
unimplemented feature to the person using it, a **broken** one.

`UNDRIVEN_TYPES` now filters the picker. A shop that already saved one keeps the
row, badged **Not supported yet**, because a device listed like every other one
that nothing drives is worse than no device at all. Building the second-screen
surface is a separate, real piece of work; withholding the promise is not.

### Quick keys — the loose half of the shop

A scanner covers everything with a barcode. It cannot cover tomatoes, rice by
the kilo, chai, or the samosas by the till — which in a mart or a dhaba are the
fastest-moving lines there are. Reaching them meant typing a name, per item, all
day.

`GET /pos/quick-keys` — **derived, never curated**. A favourites list somebody
has to maintain is wrong within a month; the till already knows what it sells.
Items with **no barcode rank first** (they are the entire reason the strip
exists), then by units sold in the window, branch-scoped. A shop's first day
returns an empty list and the strip simply isn't drawn — nothing to configure,
no empty state to explain. Cached 30 minutes: rankings move over weeks.

The strip hides the moment the cashier types in search or picks a category — a
shortlist is only useful before you have said what you want.

### Warranty claim intake — the desk can now keep what it's told

It could answer *"is this still covered?"* and then had nowhere to put the
answer. The shop's whole record of taking a battery in was what one person
remembered: the second visit started from nothing, a repeat failure looked like
a first one, and nobody could tell a supplier how many of their units came back.

`warranty_claims` + `GET/POST /warranty/claims` and `POST .../resolve`. A claim
is deliberately **not a return** — no money moves, no stock moves, and the unit
is usually gone for days; modelling it as a sale return would have put a refund
on the till that never happened.

Decisions worth keeping:

- **`resolution === null` IS the open state.** No status column beside it that
  can disagree with it.
- **Warranty is snapshotted on the day it comes in**, never recomputed. The
  window will have closed by the time a supplier replies, and a decision made in
  good faith must not read as a mistake three weeks later.
- **A serial the shop never sold can still be booked in** — a receipt from the
  other branch, a unit predating the system. Refusing just means the shop keeps
  someone's property with no record.
- **Resolved once.** A claim that can be re-resolved is one whose history can be
  rewritten after the customer was told.
- **`rejected` is a real outcome.** A shop that cannot record "this was impact
  damage, not a fault" has no defence the second time it is asked.

The desk is now two tabs: *Look up* (with every previous claim on that exact
unit, and a **Take this unit in** button) and *What we're holding* — open first,
oldest first, because the unit that has been here three weeks is the one someone
is waiting on.
